## A good file? (/a-good-file/)

- H1: A GOOD FILE ?
- H2: How should I prepare my files for printing ?

**TYPES DE FICHIERS ACCEPTÉS**
PDF (Norme PDF X/4:2010 – CMJN FOGRA39)

**BORDS PERDUS ET MISE AUX NORMES DES FICHIERS**
Add 3 mm bleed so images reach the edge after cutting. Add the bleed to the document (not inside the final format). Keep important information 4 mm away from inner margins. Crop/registration marks not necessary.

**DÉCOUPE À LA FORME**
Cut lines on a PDF layer, overprint, cyan 100% (C100 M0 J0 N0), named "Cutcontour".

**VERNIS SÉLECTIF**
Selective varnish on a layer, magenta 100% (C0 M100 J0 N0), named "Varnish".

**BLANC DE SOUTIEN**
Backing-white specification is provided with the visuals.

**GAUFRAGE, DORURE À CHAUD, DÉBOSSAGE**
Separate vectorised PDF per technique/colour in black 100% (C0 M0 J0 N100), plus a control file combining all techniques.

**BROCHURES**
Cover can be integrated in the PDF; page count must be a multiple of 4.

**ÉDITIONS**
Submit the cover separately; page count must be a multiple of 2; include a 4 mm safety margin and 3 mm bleed.
