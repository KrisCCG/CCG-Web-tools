Internal tools

Quick links to the tools we run. Internal use only. Updated 22 Oct 2026 (new shop address, new website).

Ordering & pricing

  • Shop
    Customer order flow: price, artwork upload, Mollie checkout, receipt by mail. Payments are in Mollie test mode until opening day. (login until opening day)
    clickclick.be/shop/
  • Request chat
    AI assistant: instant prices from the shop engine, hands over to the shop with the specs prefilled; other requests land in info@. (login until opening day)
    clickclick.be/chat/

Staff dashboards

  • Price calculator & Dashboard
    The internal calculator (cost and margin figures) and, in the Dashboard tab, every paid order of the shop with production status, plus browse statistics. Shows a red bar if its prices do not match the update script. (login)
    clickclick.be/calculator-v2/
  • Mail analyse
    Paste a customer email or drop an .eml from Mail: a structured quote brief with the open questions to check. (login)
    clickclick.be/mail-analyse/
  • Paper stock
    Stock levels, reorder list, offer check and admin. (login)
    clickclick.be/PaperStock/
  • MultiPress dashboard
    Turnover and orders from the MultiPress database. (login)
    clickclick.be/Dashboard-MP-CC/
