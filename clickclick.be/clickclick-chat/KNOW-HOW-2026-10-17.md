## 2. The price rules (decided by Kris)

- Online price = **Multipress price minus 10 %**, but the margin always stays at least
  **30 % on cost**. Multipress figures are always excl. VAT.
- **No online discount** on folders and brochures (Excel, Products, column `online discount` = 0 %).
- Price incl. VAT is rounded to the euro. Minimum job €50. DPD delivery €15 per order.
  Express (48 h) +35 %, pickup only.
- Paper: Excel prices, not Multipress (MultiArt prices in the Excel are the latest).
  Paper is charged at cost +50 %, always whole big sheets.
- Press: 10 min + sheets / 4140 per hour, rounded to 0.01 h, €120/h (cost €85).
- Pre-cut (MultiArt 640 × 460 cut in two): about €7.50 per print run — this is the
  "+7.50" that Multipress shows in its Digital line.
- Folding / creasing (Touchline): setup 0.10 h (1 fold), 0.15 h (2), 0.20 h (4), any paper;
  1.02 s per sheet; €110/h.
- **VAT: brochures 6 %, everything else 21 %.** Each item in the basket carries its own rate.

## 3. Changing prices — the routine

1. Change the Excel in `pricing/` (yellow cells). Write one line in the tab "Change log". Close Excel.
2. Double-click **`Update prices.command`** in `pricing/`.
   It checks every tab, calculates the test jobs, writes **8,760 test prices** (`selftest.json`)
   and fills the folder `pricing/upload/`. A report opens in the browser.
3. Upload with FileZilla: drag the two folders in `pricing/upload/` (`calculator-v2` and
   `shop-v2`) onto `/sites/clickclick.be/` and choose Overwrite.

Safety net: the shop, the chat and the calculator recalculate all 8,760 test prices before
they use a new price file. If even one differs, the shop and chat say *"prices temporarily
unavailable"* and the calculator shows a **red bar** — never a wrong price. Green bar = OK.

If the formula itself changes (not just numbers), the three engine files must be uploaded
together with the price files, otherwise the safety net blocks the prices until all are up.

## 5. Brochures (stapled booklets)

- **Only stapled (saddle stitched), always a soft cover.** No other binding and no hard cover is sold or mentioned.
- A4 closed = printed on A3; A5 closed = printed on A4 (2 per SRA3).
- **8 to 32 pages**, cover included, per 4 pages. Pages ÷ 4 = folded sheets (1 cover + the rest inside).
- **Cover = 250 g of the same paper as the inside** (MultiArt → MultiArt 250, Biotop → Biotop 250,
  RecyStar → RecyStar 250). Mixing papers is a special request (team), not in the shop.
- Always both sides, 1 version, **25 – 150 copies**, 5 days, express possible.
- **Prepress €30**, **no online discount**, **VAT 6 %**. No cutting (the BDF trims in one go).
- Printing: cover and inside are each **one print run** (more efficient than Multipress, which
  splits the inside and prints the A5 cover one per sheet).
- Cover creased like a folder (0.10 h + 1.02 s per cover).
- **Stitching** (CP Bourg BDF, cost €65/h, sell €120/h):
  - **By hand** (normal case): 0.333 h setup + copies ÷ 480 per hour.
  - **With the tower** only when **more than 75 copies AND more than 20 pages**:
    setup 0.25 h (2 sheets) … 0.30 h (12+ sheets) + 0.10 h, then the first 100 copies at
    200/h, copies 101–250 at 350/h, the rest at 500/h (smoothed, no jump like Multipress).
- Reference prices (incl. 6 % VAT): A4 16 p. ×50 = €237 · ×100 = €324 · A5 32 p. ×50 = €231 ·
  ×100 = €368 · ×150 = €456.
- Settings live in the Excel tab **Brochures**.

## 8. The chat

- **Language**: the page language decides (EN/FR/NL buttons or the site page the visitor came
  from). The chat only switches when the customer clearly writes in another language, and the
  page follows. A language picked by hand is never overruled.
- **Dutch: always "je / jij / jouw"** — never "u/uw", never "jullie" (even for a school or a
  company). **French: always "vous".**
- Prices come from the shop's own engine — the chat, the shop and the calculator always agree.
  The price card has an "Online bestellen" button that opens the shop with everything filled in.
- **Today's date**: with every message the chat gets today's date and time (Brussels) and the
  ready dates for standard and express, from the shop's own working-day rules (weekends, Belgian
  holidays, 15:00 cut-off). So it works out "Friday" itself and offers express when needed.
- **Price first**: it uses the defaults and shows a price before asking anything; the only
  question allowed before the first price is the page count of a brochure.
- Size wins: "50 cards A5" = A5, never a business-card question.
- Cards are priced **recto verso** by default; folders and brochures too; flyers and posters recto.
- Folders: roll fold ≠ Z-fold; never a page count.
- **Brochures**: the chat asks only the page count (if missing), never binding, hard/soft cover
  (always soft), cover paper or sides, and shows the shop price. Outside the shop limits → request to the team (still stapled).
- Instructions for the bot: `chat-api/system_prompt.php` (plain text inside a PHP file).
  Products and papers the bot may price are read live from the price file.
- At go-live: change `SHOP_DIR_NAME` in `chat-api/lib/shop.php` from `shop-v2` to `shop`.
