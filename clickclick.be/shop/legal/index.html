02Privacy policy

We keep this short because there is not much to tell. We do not advertise, we do not profile anyone, and we do not share data with anyone who is not needed to print and deliver your order.

No cookies, no tracking. This site sets no cookies at all and runs no analytics. The only thing stored in your browser is your language choice, so the site stays in your language between visits. That never leaves your device and needs no consent — which is why you see no cookie banner.

Fonts, scripts and images are served from our own server. No third party is contacted simply because you visited a page.

06Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your data, where we are not required to keep it — invoicing records are subject to statutory retention periods;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • receive it in a portable, machine-readable format.

Write to info@clickclickgraphics.com. We reply within one month, and it costs you nothing.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority, Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels — dataprotectionauthority.be.

07Cookies and tracking

We use no cookies. There is no analytics, no advertising pixel, no social media tracker, and no third-party font or script.

Your browser stores one thing for us: the language you chose, so the site opens in it next time. That is a functional preference, it stays on your device, and clearing your browser data removes it. No consent is required, which is why this site has no cookie banner.

08Terms of sale

These terms apply to every order placed through our online shop. Placing an order means you accept them. We are Click Click Graphics SRL, Rue d’Alost 7-11, 1000 Bruxelles, BE 0732.984.161.

Prices

All prices shown include 21% VAT. The price you see when you order is the price you pay — we do not add surcharges afterwards. Delivery within Belgium is a flat 15 € including VAT, added once per order regardless of quantity.

Ordering and payment

Your order is confirmed once payment has gone through. We ask for full payment before production because every job is made specifically for you and cannot be resold. Payment is handled by Mollie; we never see or store your card or bank details.

Your artwork

You are responsible for the file you supply — its content, spelling, colours, resolution, and your right to print it. We print what we receive.

We check files for obvious technical problems before printing — a missing bleed, a very low resolution, an odd colour mode — and we will tell you if we spot something. That is a courtesy, not a guarantee: we cannot catch everything, and we do not proofread content.

We prefer print-ready PDF, with 3 mm bleed, up to 100 MB per file. We also accept JPG, PNG and TIFF.

Production time

Standard production is 5 working days. Express is 48 hours, and is available for pick-up only — a courier cannot make 48 hours.

Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding Belgian public holidays. Orders placed after 15:00 start counting the next working day.

This is the time to produce your job, not to deliver it. The date shown at checkout is when your order leaves us.

Delivery and pick-up

Pick-up at Aalststraat 7-11 Rue d’Alost, 1000 Brussel – Bruxelles is free.

Delivery is 15 € flat within Belgium, by DPD. Transit time is added on top of production and is not within our control, so we do not promise an arrival date. You receive a tracking code when your order ships.

Outside Belgium we do ship, but the cost depends on the destination, so it is quoted by hand. Ask us and we will come back with a price.

Right of withdrawal

Everything we print is made to your specifications, from your file. Under Article VI.53, 3° of the Belgian Code of Economic Law, goods made to the consumer’s specifications or clearly personalised are exempt from the 14-day right of withdrawal.

Your order cannot be cancelled or returned once production has started. Please check your file carefully before you order.

If you contact us before we start printing, we will always try to help.

If something is wrong

Check your order when you receive or collect it, and tell us within 7 calendar days if there is a problem — email shop@clickclickgraphics.com with your invoice number and a photo.

If the fault is ours — a printing defect, the wrong specification, damage in transit — we reprint the job at our cost. This does not affect your statutory rights as a consumer under Belgian law, which we do not limit.

We cannot reprint for problems in the supplied file: a typo, a low-resolution image, or colours that were wrong in the artwork.

Colour

Screens are backlit and printers are not. Small colour differences between what you see on screen and what comes off the press are normal and are not a defect. If exact colour matters, ask us about a proof before ordering.

Liability

We are liable for damage caused by our own fault. Our liability is limited to the value of the order concerned, except where the law does not permit such a limit — in particular for death, personal injury, or intent.

Applicable law and disputes

Belgian law applies. We would always rather sort a problem out directly, so please write to us first. Consumers may also contact the Belgian Consumer Mediation Service, Boulevard du Roi Albert II 8, 1000 Brussels. Disputes fall under the courts of Brussels.
